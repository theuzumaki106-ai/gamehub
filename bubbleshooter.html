Orb Burst

Aim, shoot, and match 3 or more orbs of the same color to burst them. Clear the field before the orbs reach the bottom line!

Drag or move your pointer to aim, tap or click to fire. Match 3+ same-colored orbs to pop them and drop anything left floating. Tap the "Next" orb to swap it with your loaded shot.
