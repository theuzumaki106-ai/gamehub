📖 How to Play & Rules

Rules & Pro Tips
🎯

Aim & Shoot

Move mouse cursor or drag your finger to aim the dashed laser guide (it bounces off side walls!). Click or release touch to fire your orb.

💥

Match 3+ to Pop

Connect 3 or more orbs of the same color to pop them. Clear the whole field to win the level!

🪂

Drop Detached Orbs (+15 Bonus)

Any orbs that lose connection to the top ceiling after a match will fall off the screen, awarding huge bonus points!

⇄

Swap Orbs [Space]

Need a different color? Click the Next Orb preview in the HUD or press the [Space] key to swap the loaded orb.

⚠️

Ceiling Drop Warning (Foul Dots)

Notice the 5 dots ● ● ● ● ● below the board. Making a match never drops the ceiling! The ceiling only drops if you miss 5 shots in a row.

⛔

Dashed Danger Line

Keep orbs above the red dashed line at the bottom. If any orb crosses this line, it's Game Over!

🌟
